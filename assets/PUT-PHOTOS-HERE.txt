WHAT GOES IN THIS FOLDER
========================

1) THE MAIN PHOTO  →  photo.jpg
   The big circular portrait with the party hat.
   Square-ish crop works best. ~800x800, under ~500 KB.

2) THE MEMORY GALLERY  →  memory-1.jpg, memory-2.jpg, memory-3.jpg, memory-4.jpg
   These become the tilted polaroids further down the card.
   Square-ish again, ~800x800 each. Tap one and it opens full size.

   Want more or fewer? Open index.html and edit the `gallery:` list in the
   CARD block near the top of the <script>. Any photo listed there that
   doesn't exist just quietly disappears, so you can leave extras in the
   list without breaking anything. Add captions there too:

       gallery: [
         { src: "assets/memory-1.jpg", caption: "Brighton, 2023" },
         { src: "assets/memory-2.jpg", caption: "the good pizza place" }
       ]

   If you add NO gallery photos, the whole section hides itself.


FILE TYPES
----------
Use .jpg. If yours are .png or .heic, either convert them, or change the
filenames in the CARD block to match what you actually have.

Until photos are here, the card falls back to her first initial and simply
skips the gallery — nothing ever looks broken.

You can delete this text file once your photos are in place.
