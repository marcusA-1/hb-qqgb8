Drop the birthday girl's photo in this folder, named exactly:

    photo.jpg

That's it — the card picks it up automatically, no code changes needed.

Tips:
  - A square-ish crop looks best (the frame is a circle).
  - Aim for roughly 800x800 px and under ~500 KB so it loads fast on phones.
  - If your file is a PNG or HEIC, either rename/convert it to photo.jpg,
    or open index.html and change the `photo:` line in the CARD block
    near the top of the <script> to match your filename.

Until a photo is here, the card gracefully shows her first initial instead,
so nothing looks broken.

You can delete this text file once the photo is in place.
